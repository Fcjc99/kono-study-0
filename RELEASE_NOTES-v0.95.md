# KONO v0.95 — Living Sanctuary Foundation

This release activates the Sanctuary as a real Phaser world inside the production dashboard.

## Highlights

- Smooth dawn, midday, dusk, and night transitions
- No black flash or abrupt image replacement
- Layered world rendering and anchored lighting
- Organic randomized ripples, petals, leaves, sparkles, and fireflies
- Clear, Cloudy, Rain, Breezy, and Snow states
- Rain-aware pond behavior
- Mobile performance scaling
- Reduced Motion support
- Hidden developer time/weather preview panel
- Growth hooks for v0.98
- Phase-aware cassette ambience label and volume

## Testing

Use `?sanctuaryDebug=1` to preview every time and weather combination immediately.

## Companion

No companion was added. The custom KONO character remains reserved for v1.0.
