
# Build 22.1.8 — Terrace Evening/Night PNG Remake

This pass remakes the evening and night terrace PNGs directly from the approved afternoon terrace silhouettes.

## Goal
Remove the overlay-like evening look. The terrace should read as a single clean PNG silhouette with phase-appropriate lighting baked into the art.

## Changes
- Rebuilt terrace stages 1-5 for **evening** from the afternoon silhouette with a gentle dusk grade and controlled warm lantern lighting baked into the PNG.
- Rebuilt terrace stages 1-5 for **night** from the afternoon silhouette with a moonlit dark grade and the terrace lights turned off.
- No runtime terrace glow/emissive overlays are used; lighting is carried by the phase PNG itself.
