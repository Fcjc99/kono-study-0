# Plateau Evolution — Proposed 0–5 Path

- Stage 0 — Open Plateau: current grassy top with stairs; quiet and uncluttered.
- Stage 1 — First Marker: a small stone marker / tiny garden accent appears near the center while the plateau stays open.
- Stage 2 — Resting Nook: add a compact bench or meditation stone, a few flowers, and subtle path definition.
- Stage 3 — Blossom Terrace: add controlled flower beds / stone edging and a small lantern pair around the central tree zone.
- Stage 4 — Sanctuary Terrace: richer stonework, balanced planting, lanterns, and a more intentional approach from the stairs.
- Stage 5 — Summit Sanctuary: polished but cozy final terrace with refined stone border, seating / contemplation detail, flowers, lanterns, and subtle ambient accents; the cherry tree remains the visual centerpiece.

Rules: keep the current plateau footprint, stairs, tree position, and camera orientation; grow through details rather than large structures; use pixel-art PNG assets only.
