About

Kit that looks exactly like you designed it

THREADLAB makes custom athletic and team wear to order. You design on true-to-life 3D garments, and we print, screen or stitch exactly what you see.

How it works

  1. Design. Pick a garment and colour it panel by panel, add patterns, text, graphics and your logos.
  2. Add your team. Paste a roster and every piece gets its own name, number and size.
  3. Review. Check every side and the full spec before it goes in your bag.
  4. Order. Send your order request. We confirm the details, send an invoice and start production.

Decoration and pricing

Questions

Is there a minimum order?

No for digital print and embroidery. Screen print starts at 12 pieces, because each colour needs its own screen.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the order and the decoration. We confirm a production timeline with your order or quote before anything is made.

Can I see a sample first?

For team orders, yes. Ask in a team quote.

What files do you need for my logo?

A PNG with a transparent background works for most things. For screen print and embroidery, vector files (SVG or PDF) give the cleanest result.
